# Awesome-Probation-Management

## Top Probation Management Platforms Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Probation & Parole Case Management, Electronic Monitoring, Offender Supervision, Risk Assessment & Community Corrections*  

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Probation Management**. These tools help probation and parole agencies manage caseloads, track compliance, support electronic monitoring, conduct risk/needs assessments, schedule contacts, and improve outcomes for individuals under community supervision.



**Examples** include Corrisoft, BI TotalAccess, SuperCom, Pioneer Technology Group, Equivant Probation, Omnigo, Tyler Supervision, Judicial Innovations, TrackGroup, and Attenti (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for case management, sentence planning, community corrections tools, and related open justice technology — ideal for agencies, researchers, and developers seeking transparent or self-hosted alternatives in the sensitive domain of offender supervision.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Corrisoft](https://www.corrisoft.com/)**  

  Electronic monitoring and offender supervision solutions combining GPS devices, smartphone apps, and case management tools for probation and parole agencies.



- **[BI TotalAccess / BI Incorporated](https://bi.com/)**  

  Comprehensive electronic monitoring and community supervision platform offering GPS, RF, and alcohol monitoring integrated with case management.



- **[SuperCom](https://www.supercom.com/)**  

  Global provider of electronic monitoring hardware and software (PureSecurity suite) for probation, parole, and community corrections programs.



- **[Pioneer Technology Group](https://www.pioneertechnology.com/)**  

  Justice and public safety software solutions that include probation and case management capabilities for courts and community supervision agencies.



- **[Equivant Probation](https://www.equivant.com/)**  

  Case management and supervision tools focused on probation, pretrial, and community corrections workflows.



- **[Omnigo](https://www.omnigo.com/)**  

  Public safety and incident management platform that supports probation and community supervision operations.



- **[Tyler Supervision](https://www.tylertech.com/products/tyler-supervision)**  

  Enterprise cloud-based supervision case management system for pretrial, adult and juvenile probation, and parole agencies.



- **[Judicial Innovations](https://www.judicialinnovations.com/)**  

  Technology solutions tailored for courts and justice agencies, including supervision and case tracking features.



- **[TrackGroup](https://www.trackgrp.com/)**  

  End-to-end electronic monitoring and location-based supervision solutions with analytics for criminal justice agencies.



- **[Attenti](https://www.attentigroup.com/)**  

  Electronic monitoring and offender management technology provider serving probation, parole, and corrections markets worldwide.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[HMPPS Sentence Plan UI (UK Ministry of Justice)](https://github.com/ministryofjustice/hmpps-sentence-plan-ui)**  

  Open-source sentence planning interface developed for the UK Probation Service to support structured rehabilitation planning for individuals under supervision.



- **[BC Community Corrections Case Management (CCCM)](https://github.com/bcgov/jag-community-corrections-case-management)**  

  Open-source community corrections case management system developed by the Government of British Columbia for probation and related supervision workflows.



- **[Justice OS](https://github.com/dougdevitre/justice-os)**  

  Modular open-source platform for building justice applications, including case management cores and shared infrastructure for legal and corrections technology.



- **[OSCaR – Open Source Case Management](https://github.com/Children-in-Families/oscar-web)**  

  Open-source case management and reporting system originally developed for social services that can be adapted for structured supervision and case tracking.



- **[LawLink](https://github.com/lawflow-boop/LawLink)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted case and practice management system that provides a foundation for case tracking, notes, and workflow management adaptable to justice contexts.



- **[WKS Platform](https://github.com/wkspower/wks-platform)**  

  Open-source adaptive case management platform built on Camunda, suitable for complex, unstructured case workflows including supervision processes.



- **[Foreman (Forensic Case Management)](https://github.com/ubunteroz/foreman)**  

  Open-source forensic case management system focused on organizing cases, tasks, evidence, and audit trails.



- **[Parole & Probation Field Tools](https://github.com/agrc/parole-and-probation)**  

  Open-source web applications developed for parole officers to support visit planning and field supervision activities.



- **[General Case Management Frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Broader open-source case management engines and low-code platforms that agencies have adapted for probation and community corrections use cases.



- **[Risk/Needs Assessment Prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Academic and research open-source implementations of risk assessment instruments and decision-support tools used in community supervision.



- **[Electronic Monitoring Data Processors](https://github.com/)**  

  Community scripts and tools for processing GPS/location data and generating compliance reports (typically requiring integration with commercial devices).



- **[Court & Justice Integration Projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source modules for exchanging data between courts, probation, and other justice system components using standards-based approaches.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Government-led projects**: UK HMPPS Sentence Plan and BC CCCM represent some of the most relevant production-oriented open efforts in community corrections.

- **Modular justice platforms**: Justice OS and similar initiatives aim to create reusable building blocks for justice technology.

- **Case management cores**: General open-source case management systems that can be customized for supervision workflows.

- **Research tools**: Academic projects focused on risk assessment, recidivism prediction, and rehabilitation planning.

- The domain remains dominated by commercial vendors; truly open-source full probation management suites are still rare.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine open **case management platforms** (WKS, Justice OS, or adapted social-service systems), sentence/rehabilitation planning modules, secure data storage, and integration layers for electronic monitoring devices to prototype or extend supervision capabilities. Production use in justice settings requires rigorous security, auditability, and legal compliance review.
